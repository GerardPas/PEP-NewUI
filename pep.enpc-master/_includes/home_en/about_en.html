Who we are

Ponts Études Projets is a student association of École Nationale des Ponts et Chaussées, part of the Junior Enterprise movement for more than 45 years. Our fields of expertise, inherited from the teaching at our school, allow us to support companies large and small in delivering their projects.

The breadth of the programmes on offer, the presence of research laboratories and of international students let us bring the best resources to every assignment. Year after year, our Junior Enterprise sharpens its expertise in the ecological and sustainable transition, digital transformation, and strategy and decision support.
