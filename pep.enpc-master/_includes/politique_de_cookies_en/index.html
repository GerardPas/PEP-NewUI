Scope of the cookie policy :

As some cookies may process personal data, we recommend that you take a few minutes to read our Cookie Policy.

What are cookies ?

A cookie is a small text file that is stored by your browser on your device, such as your computer or mobile phone, when you visit a website and can send information back to that site. This information is most often used to facilitate your navigation, for example by remembering your choice of language.
However, cookies may be used for a variety of purposes, such as collecting statistics about the way you use a site, enhancing site security or displaying information correctly.

Do we use cookies?

We use cookies on several pages of our Site and this section indicates the different types of cookies we use and for what purposes.

Strictly necessary cookies
These cookies are essential to the operation of the Website and do not require your consent. They are used in particular to store your cookie choice, so that you are not asked again on every page.

Audience measurement cookies
We use Google Analytics, an audience analysis service provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand how our Website is used — sections and content visited, browsing paths — and to improve our services. These cookies are loaded through Google Tag Manager.
They produce pseudonymous traffic statistics: an identifier is assigned to your browser, without us seeking to identify you by name. They are only set after your consent and their lifetime does not exceed 13 months.
For further information on how Google handles this data, please visit : https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245

Other cookies
We maintain several social media platforms and offer users the ability to join us on these platforms or post content by clicking on the social media icons on our Website. Moreover, some of the Website's pages deliver our content through the embedding of videos from these social media platforms.
To provide these services, cookies from social media platforms, such as Twitter, Google and YouTube, may be used on some of our web pages. The videos we embed are loaded from the youtube-nocookie.com domain: YouTube then sets no cookie unless you start playback yourself.
If you have any questions about the use of third-party cookies, we recommend that you read the cookie policies published on the websites of these platforms.

How to manage cookies?

Audience measurement cookies are only set after your explicit consent. On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline them; both options are offered with equal ease, and no measurement cookie is set before you click “Accept”.
Until you answer, or if you decline, audience measurement stays switched off. Declining has no effect on how the site works or on access to its content.
You can change your mind at any time by clicking “Manage cookies” at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your consent, measurement is switched off immediately and cookies already set are deleted.
You can also delete all cookies already stored by your browser, or set it to alert you to cookies stored on your device. If you decide to delete your cookies, please be aware that any preferences you may have set will be lost.

Declining an audience cookie
To decline audience measurement, click “Decline” in the banner on your first visit, or “Manage cookies” at the bottom of the page at any time afterwards. No third-party step and no software installation is required.
Your choice is stored in your browser. It is therefore specific to each browser and each device, and it will be forgotten if you clear your browsing data — the banner will then be shown to you again.

Caution, these cookies allow us to measure the traffic or audience, associated with our Website, the pages visited and the interactions carried out on our site during your visit. Disabling them therefore prevents any collection of information relating to your browsing on our Website and therefore the proposal of editorial content adapted according to your browsing.
